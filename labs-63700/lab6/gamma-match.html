How to use this page

  1. Set the browser zoom to 100% (Cmd+0 on a Mac, Ctrl+0 on Windows).
  2. The picture below has tall bands side by side. Each band labeled lines is made of black and white lines. Half of its lines are white, so on average it is half as bright as white. Each band labeled gray is one solid gray, with gray level g.
  3. Step back from the screen until you can no longer see the separate lines.
  4. Move the slider until the lines bands and the gray bands look equally bright, so that the bands seem to disappear. Write down g.
  5. Your display gamma is γ = log(0.5) / log(g/255). For the quarter pattern, one line in four is white, so use 0.25 in place of 0.5.

Magnifier

Move the mouse over the pattern.
Click to hold the view.

The magnifier shows 16 by 16 display pixels around the mouse, each drawn as a 15 by 15 square, with the gray value of each pixel in the middle column listed on the right. In a pattern band you should see the black and white lines repeat with the line width you chose.

The magnifier shows the pixel values this page sends to the display. To see what the display itself shows, take a screenshot of the pattern (Cmd+Shift+4 on a Mac, Windows+Shift+S on Windows) and zoom in on it. A screenshot records the actual display pixels, so the lines in it should be sharp, with the same width and spacing as in the magnifier.
